# Pilot support runbook

## Intake

The guest-capable support centre separates emergency help, housing guidance, technical support, accessibility, privacy, safeguarding and complaints. Reporters can choose anonymous submission and an optional safe contact. Staff listings omit encrypted descriptions and contacts.

Categories are technical, accessibility, housing content, incorrect service information, privacy, security, safeguarding, complaint, feature request, account access, evidence problem and consent/sharing. Response targets are one hour for critical, four hours for high, 24 hours for material and 72 hours for standard reports. These targets are operational expectations, not emergency guarantees.

## Safeguarding

Safeguarding reports generate a distinct escalation audit event. A named safeguarding lead must monitor the queue during approved support hours with deputy and out-of-hours arrangements. Immediate danger is always directed to 999; urgent housing help is directed to the council emergency route. Never rely on a generic unmonitored inbox.

## Handling

1. Verify the safe-contact instruction before replying.
2. Assign only to staff whose active role covers the pilot.
3. Keep case narratives and evidence contents out of operational notes.
4. Link protected records rather than copying their contents.
5. Escalate privacy/security issues to incident command when thresholds are met.
6. Record resolution, communicate safely and close only after the reporter-facing action is complete.

Anonymous tickets cannot be used to infer identity. Non-anonymous complaints may receive a tracking reference where safe. Support staff cannot activate features, publish dataset changes or obtain unrestricted case access.
