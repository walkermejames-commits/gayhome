# Data correction workflow

Users, support staff and partners may report wrong contact details, broken links, closures, hours, eligibility, age, coverage, accessibility, council routes or safety concerns. A proposal preserves record type and ID, current value, proposed value, reason, evidence, urgency and submitter scope.

Partners and users never publish. A data reviewer investigates the source and either rejects the proposal with a reason or marks it verified. Critical service references may be temporarily hidden only by an authorised incident/reviewer action; that action is audited and does not overwrite the controlled dataset.

Publication occurs through the existing dataset import/revision pipeline. After a new revision is validated, approved, published and active, a reviewer may link the verified proposal to that revision. The proposal then records reviewer, decision, dataset revision and publication time. The previous revision remains available for provenance and rollback.

Unsupported proposals are rejected, not deleted. Direct mutation of a live service or council row from partner APIs is not implemented and is prohibited. Rollback means selecting an approved earlier dataset revision through the governed dataset process, not reversing individual fields without history.
