# Outcome measurement

Outcome reporting is optional. The system records a controlled outcome type, date, region, source and optional pilot/cohort/participant references. It does not ask for a narrative and must not rank, pressure or determine support for a participant.

Supported indicators include correct council, applications prepared/submitted, references obtained, specialist contact, emergency accommodation, eviction prevention, housing plan, adjustments, gatekeeping challenge, review, advocate involvement, next-step understanding and confidence.

Commissioner reports aggregate by controlled labels and period. Counts below five are suppressed to `null`; the minimum may be raised but not lowered. Reports exclude names, exact addresses, case narratives, LGBTQIA+ identity, health, abuse, immigration and evidence contents. Outputs distinguish actual data, targets, assumptions, projections and unmeasured outcomes.

Optional reporting and small-cell suppression mean totals are incomplete. Reports must state these limits. Region/period filters and pseudonymous event references support operational analysis without creating a user-ranking dataset.
