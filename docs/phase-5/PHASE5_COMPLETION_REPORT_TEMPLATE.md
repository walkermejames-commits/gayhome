# Phase 5 completion report template

Status: **Not eligible for completion. Phase 4 and operational entry gates are blocked.**

Report only measured, aggregate evidence. Keep projections, assumptions and targets separate from actual outcomes.

## Phase 4 verification

Record source branch, immutable completion commit, merge base, integration decision, exact test results, defects, fixes and independent review.

## Release gates

For every canonical gate record status, owner, evidence, decision, remaining risk and pilot/production/expansion effect.

## Pilot

Record cohorts, aggregate users, enabled/disabled features, duration, incidents, findings, changes and outcomes. Do not include case narratives or small-cell identity combinations.

## Production and operations

Record isolated environment, immutable version, dataset revision, release date/approver, rollback version, monitoring, support, safeguarding, editorial, data-verification, incident and recovery ownership.

## Data, security and accessibility

Record services/councils reverified, corrections/revision, penetration test, findings/remediation, manual accessibility/browser/screen-reader/disabled-user evidence and remaining defects.

## Outcomes and sustainability

Record optional privacy-safe applications/referrals/routes/usefulness/repeated-question outcomes; staffing and cost scenarios; actual funding; partner model; revenue safeguards; and operating assumptions. Never claim unmeasured outcomes or monetise crisis ranking.

## Expansion and limitations

Record regional isolation, Sussex templates and `SUSSEX-DATA-001`. List every unfinished feature, open gate, missing owner, unsupported journey, unverified record and operational dependency.
