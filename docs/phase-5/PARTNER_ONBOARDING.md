# Partner onboarding

Partner organisations record type, region, accountable primary contact, safeguarding contact and data-protection contact. Contact details are encrypted. Agreement, training and verification states are independent so training alone never grants access.

Operational onboarding requires:

1. verified organisation identity and represented services;
2. approved current participation/data agreement;
3. named safeguarding and privacy contacts;
4. privacy, consent, accessibility, safeguarding and correction-workflow training;
5. an approved, time-limited membership and operational role;
6. suspension and revocation routes tested.

Partners may review their organisation, confirm operational facts and submit correction proposals. They cannot publish, search users, view unrelated cases, access private user data without consent, change release gates or activate features. Partner scope is checked server-side by organisation membership. PHASE5-PARTNER-001 remains open until an independent reviewer tests escalation, cross-organisation access, suspension and revocation.
