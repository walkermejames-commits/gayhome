# Phase 6 operations and reporting

## Verification

Verification stores separate confidence for contact details, hours, eligibility, coverage, accessibility, LGBTQIA+ inclusion, accommodation availability and referral route. Scheduling weights urgency, service risk, change history, unstable contacts, funding/seasonality, reports, broken links, attestation age, council state and safeguarding importance. Queue views must include due today, seven-day, overdue, critical overdue, conflicting and safety-report categories.

Critical changes require a dependency impact report covering routes, plans, scripts, guidance, pages, offline cache, forms, partner documents and public cards. It reports only an aggregate potential-user count, never identities, and includes revalidation plus rollback.

## Reporting and gaps

Commissioner/reporting roles have no case entitlement. Reports require period, dataset revision, limitations, actual/projection labels and a minimum cell of five. Gap language distinguishes observed, possible, data-quality, verification, capacity and geographic issues and never treats a missing database record as proof of no service.

## Costs and funders

Cost scenarios cover internal development, controlled pilot, Kent beta, full Kent and Medway, Sussex expansion and multi-region service. Assumptions remain editable with value, currency, date, source, confidence and notes; no market price is permanent truth. Funder claims are individually labelled implemented, in development, planned, tested, approved or not yet measured. No user outcomes are inferred.

## Notifications and media

Notification records include purpose, sensitivity, channel allowlist, variables, three wording modes, approval and translation state. Lock-screen wording contains no case detail. Media records are placeholders only, with transcript/captions/audio-description/version/review/expiry fields; no BSL video is fabricated or treated as the sole emergency source.
